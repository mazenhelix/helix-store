Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Helix Studio (helixstore.dpdns.org) is an independent studio selling Minecraft animation templates and offering custom commissions through the Helix Showcase.

What we collect

If you sign in with Google on the Showcase, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to identify your account, show your name to the editors you commission, and let you use the dashboard. We also store what you submit: commission requests, chat messages, reviews, editor applications and portfolio entries.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads or share your information with third parties, except the services that run this site: Cloudflare (hosting and database), Google (sign-in), Formspree (the store commission form) and Payhip (payments for templates, which handles its own payment data).

Who can see your data

Your commission requests and chats are visible to you, the editor involved and the site owner. Your display name appears to editors you contact. Reviews and portfolio entries you publish are public.

Deleting your data
