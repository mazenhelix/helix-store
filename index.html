How do I install a template in Premiere Pro?

Open Premiere Pro, go to Window > Essential Graphics, click the Install Motion Graphics Template button at the bottom right of the panel, and pick your downloaded .mogrt file. It then shows up under Local Templates, ready to drag onto your timeline.

Which Premiere Pro version do I need?

These templates were made in 2024, so you need Premiere Pro 2024 (version 24) or newer.

Do I need After Effects?

No. Each template is a ready-made .mogrt file that you edit inside Premiere Pro (text, images and colors in the Essential Graphics panel).

Does it work on Mac and Windows?

Yes. .mogrt files work on both, as long as your Premiere Pro version is supported.
